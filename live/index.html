Two tracks, one long day

There are primarily two tracks today: expedition (analysis) and building (coding). Everything lives in the meta issue, aigroup #44.

01 / expedition · everyone should try it

If this is the only thing you do today, that's fine; it's worth it. It can easily take up the whole day, and each item in it can too. Two ways in:

  • Meander. Open the notebook provided (details in #44) and wander around. It works wherever you have Python and a network connection, but it will download a fair bit of data.
  • Follow a recipe. Work through the five curated exercises in the sub-issues of #44, with or without your agents.

03 / feeling super adventurous · huggingface

The Hugging Face repos hold a lot beyond the obvious, including instructions to reproduce everything. There is a ~230 GB dataset of latent details (it lets you do the expedition without ever touching the underlying models) and a few GB of checkpoints for two real models we are testing now. New to Hugging Face? Ask any time today, or ask your agent; it knows this stuff far better than any of us.

Most importantly: please help us improve the repo and the code. Even half-baked thoughts or ranty complaints help a lot. Write down your impressions, your feelings, all of it.
